Why Educators Switch to PocketSens

Built for Real K–12 Classrooms, Not Just Lab Benches

We interviewed STEM teachers to eliminate the three biggest headaches in micro:bit classrooms: broken battery wires, tippy vertical connectors, and blind sensor logging.

🛡️

Integrated Insulated Battery Case

Say goodbye to dangling JST battery packs that snap off in student hands. PocketSens features a dedicated molded plastic battery enclosure permanently bonded to the PCB with industrial thermal insulation tape and protected by an onboard SMT PPTC resettable fuse.

📐

180° Flat-Lay Ergonomic Slot

Vertical edge connectors wobble and strain gold fingers. Our 180-degree horizontal slot lays completely flat on school desks, keeping both the micro:bit LED matrix and the 1.8" Color TFT display facing straight up for effortless viewing.

📊

One-Block Live Data Graphing

Students can walk around the schoolyard measuring light, temperature, sound, or I2C sensors and watch real-time scrolling line charts auto-scale on the color TFT screen—no serial monitor or laptop tether required.
